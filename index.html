About this project

Álvaro Hernández
Finance & Accounting · UC3M Madrid · Class of 2027

FINscope is a full-stack financial intelligence platform demonstrating how modern tooling can deliver institutional-grade equity analysis at near-zero marginal cost.

The backend pulls Finnhub, Alpha Vantage, Yahoo Finance and FRED in parallel. Computes Altman Z-Score, Piotroski F-Score and a composite score with a transparent rule-by-rule breakdown, then drafts an 11-section report through two parallel OpenAI calls anchored on a sector-specific WACC benchmark table.

Charts from TradingView. Watchlist alerts via Resend + Vercel cron. Informational only — not investment advice.
